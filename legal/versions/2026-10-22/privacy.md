# Privacy Policy

**Effective: 22 October 2026**

Bracket Control is operated by a New Zealand sole trader trading as **Bracket Control** and **Dev Dyl NZ** (NZBN **9429054041851**). For privacy matters contact **privacy@devdylnz.com**; for other legal matters **legal@devdylnz.com**.

## Information we handle

To operate Discord-integrated tournaments we handle Discord IDs and display names, server membership and permissions, gaming tags, team names, captains, rosters, draft selections, weekly attendance, brackets, scores, rankings, match results, disputes, scoresheet files and audit/notification records. Discord sign-in also involves encrypted OAuth tokens and temporary sessions. Configured draft waiting rooms may provide voice-presence information.

You do not normally have to provide a real name, home address, phone number, birth date, identity document or payment card. Uploaded files and names chosen by users may nevertheless contain additional information; please avoid submitting unnecessary personal details.

## How we obtain and use information

Captains and organisers may enter teammates' gaming tags and optional Discord IDs even if teammates never sign in. We also receive information through Discord, authorised sign-in, tournament interactions and privacy requests. We use this information to administer competitions, verify permissions and match reports, resolve disputes, publish results, maintain records, secure and recover the service, and answer requests.

Captains and organisers should inform affected players promptly and share our [Player Information Notice]({{ '/legal/player-notice/' | relative_url }}). We also remain responsible for applicable requirements when information is collected indirectly.

Providing necessary registration details is voluntary, but an organiser may be unable to enter someone without a usable gaming tag.

## Public tournament information

Team names, gaming tags, approved team rosters, brackets, standings, results and historical placements **may be published** on authorised Bracket Control pages and Discord channels selected by organisers. Website tournament visibility is separate from Discord announcement settings. Pending roster changes and private match evidence are not intended as public rosters.

Independent Discord copies and screenshots may continue to exist after something changes on our platform. Avoid placing contact details or sensitive information in public team names, gaming tags or announcements.

## Service providers and overseas handling

Discord supports account and bot integration; Cloudflare supports web delivery and certain storage; Railway hosts application and database infrastructure; GitHub Pages hosts documentation. Email providers handle correspondence. These providers may process data outside New Zealand. Authorised captains and organisers can see information needed for their roles, while spectators may see information deliberately made public. We do not sell players' information or deliberately expose one community's private records to another.

## Security, cookies and storage

We use access controls, session security and encrypted OAuth tokens. The [Cookie Notice]({{ '/legal/cookies/' | relative_url }}) describes essential authentication cookies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain information for legitimate tournament operation, history, disputes, accountability, security, recovery and legal obligations. Historical results may be retained where justified. Conditional scoresheet cleanup exists in software for eligible files **no earlier than 90 days after tournament completion**, but is subject to holds and configuration and **may not be active in production**. This is not a promise that all files or backups disappear on day 90. Provider logs, backups and independent Discord copies have different handling.

## Privacy rights

Email **privacy@devdylnz.com** to ask for access, correction, or consideration of deletion, or to report a concern. Include a server or gaming tag if useful, but do not email passwords or identity documents. We may need to verify identity and another person's privacy before responding. Requests are handled under applicable law. You can contact the [New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/).

## Age policy

Bracket Control's product minimum is **13+**, or a higher minimum if required by local law or Discord's rules. Additional protections may apply. Discord authentication does not prove a person's age; we do not routinely collect birth dates or identity documents for tournament participation. Report a concern to privacy@devdylnz.com.

## Updates and contact

We will date and explain relevant changes. Rules applying to specific overseas markets may require further legal assessment; this policy is not a certification of worldwide compliance.
