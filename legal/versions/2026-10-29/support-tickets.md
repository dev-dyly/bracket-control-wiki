# Support Ticket Privacy Disclosure

**Published and effective: 29 October 2026** | Version `support-tickets-2026-10-29-v1`

Server support tickets are private to their creator and authorised support personnel. On closure we preserve an encrypted transcript for the server's configured period, normally 30 days (1–90 days). You may access your retained transcripts using your authenticated Discord account even after leaving a server. Closed Discord channels may be removed sooner, normally after 24 hours, once evidence is preserved and no hold prevents removal. If you separately allow support ticket notifications, we attempt a private Discord closure link; no conversation content is included. You may withdraw that permission on My Support Tickets. Delivery may fail, and your website access does not depend on successful delivery. Access ends at expiry; legal preservation may delay content destruction. Audit records and provider/backup copies follow their applicable retention and privacy procedures.
