# Privacy Policy

**Published and effective: 24 October 2026** · Version `2026-10-24`

Bracket Control is operated by **Dev Dyl NZ — Trading as Bracket Control**, a New Zealand sole trader (NZBN **9429054041851**). Dev Dyl NZ and Bracket Control are trading names of the same operator, not separate legal entities.

Business website: [devdylnz.com](https://devdylnz.com). Application: [app.devdylnz.com/app/](https://app.devdylnz.com/app/). Privacy: [privacy@devdylnz.com](mailto:privacy@devdylnz.com). Legal: [legal@devdylnz.com](mailto:legal@devdylnz.com).

## Information we handle

We handle information needed to operate Discord-integrated tournaments: Discord User IDs, usernames, display names or server nicknames, available avatars, relevant membership and permissions; gaming tags, teams, captain ownership, rosters and revisions; draft waiting-room presence and picks; attendance, divisions, brackets, scores, standings, results and disputes; permitted match evidence and scoresheets; and security, administration and recovery records. Discord sign-in involves encrypted OAuth tokens and temporary sessions.

Agreement evidence identifies the authenticated captain, hosting server, tournament, team, exact rules and legal-document versions, declaration wording and revision, timestamp and roster fingerprint. Privacy-notification records include collection context, attempts and outcomes, optional contact permission, captain/organiser follow-up declarations and optional player receipt acknowledgement. This feature does not add IP-address or device-fingerprint collection for agreement evidence.

We do not routinely ask for legal names, residential addresses, phone numbers, birth dates, identity documents or payment cards. Do not put unnecessary personal or sensitive information in tags, team names, files or messages.

## Sources and purposes

Information may come directly from you, Discord, team captains, tournament organisers, authorised administrators and imports, configured Discord draft waiting rooms, or recurring registration processes. Players can be listed without signing in. Waiting-room discovery uses relevant account and voice-presence information; this feature does not record voice conversations.

We use necessary information for registration and account verification, tournament and draft administration, roster approval, attendance, match reporting, results and standings, disputes, privacy notifications and requests, agreement evidence, security and accountability, storage and recovery. A captain's declaration is not a blanket legal basis for every use. We assess our own responsibilities when information is supplied by someone else.

## Discord player selection

For new captain-managed submissions in explicitly activated tournaments, the captain's signed-in Discord account is associated automatically. The captain searches the hosting server by Discord username or nickname, selects each teammate and the application fills their display name or gaming tag and associates their Discord User ID. The gaming tag can be edited independently.

Name search may not find global display names or gaming tags. Manual Discord User ID entry is a fallback; the backend verifies eligible membership in the hosting server and rechecks submitted identities. A plausible number alone is not verification. If verification fails, the captain-managed submission cannot proceed for that player until resolved through an authorised organiser process.

These are product requirements for that activated workflow, not a law requiring manual ID entry. Historical or inactive registrations and authorised organiser/import/draft workflows retain their applicable policies and may lack IDs. Verification establishes relevant account membership, not real-world identity, age, legal capacity or contractual consent. A stable ID preserves account associations when usernames change.

## Tournament rules and declarations

Platform [Terms of Service](https://docs.devdylnz.com/legal/terms/) are separate from each tournament's rules. Authorised organisers publish complete authoritative text during tournament creation. Revisions preserve earlier versions. Supplementary HTTPS references are not captured or independently verified external content.

The captain's single declaration records their own acceptance and what they supplied to, and were told by, teammates. It does not record separate teammate acceptance. Recurring occurrences have their own rules snapshots; template updates do not rewrite existing occurrences. Unchanged declarations can be reused only within verified applicable scope. Changed rules, roster, captain ownership or legal versions may require renewed review. Registration, sporting approval and weekly attendance remain separate.

## Privacy notifications

Where enabled, collection creates a durable private notice with the relevant server, tournament, team or draft context, source and document references. A matching authenticated Discord account can access its in-app notice. Availability does not prove receipt, and historical records are not automatically backfilled.

Discord privacy DMs require the recipient's own explicit authenticated permission, separately enabled delivery and permitted provider access. Captains cannot opt teammates in. Contact permission can be withdrawn through the account's privacy controls for future attempts; this does not remove an already sent message or automatically resend earlier unavailable notices. Discord privacy DMs are disabled for this initial release.

Blocks, opt-outs, rate limits and provider failures can prevent delivery. Uncertain sends require review. Captains or authorised organisers may supply privacy information through an appropriate existing channel and declare that they did so; this is not independently verified delivery. Dev Dyl NZ oversees outstanding notifications and retains its own applicable duties. We do not claim every player is automatically contacted successfully.

Players may optionally acknowledge receiving privacy information using their own authenticated account. This records receipt, versions and time, not contractual acceptance or DM permission. Teammate login, acknowledgement and DM permission are not prerequisites for captain registration.

## Access, recipients and public information

Authorised captains and organisers access information appropriate to their roles and community. The operator and authorised support/privacy personnel access what is needed to run the service and handle requests. Private records are protected by account, role and community controls.

Team names, gaming tags, approved rosters, brackets, scores, standings, results and historical placements may be public on authorised tournament pages and selected Discord channels, including to spectators outside the server. Website visibility and Discord publishing are separate. Pending roster proposals and private match evidence are not intended as public rosters. This feature does not publish Discord IDs, private notices or agreement/delivery histories on public tournament pages. Independent screenshots and Discord copies may remain outside our control.

## Providers and overseas handling

Discord provides account, server and integration services; Cloudflare supports web delivery and certain storage; Railway hosts application/database infrastructure; GitHub Pages hosts documentation; and email, security and recovery providers support those functions. Providers may process information outside New Zealand and their own practices may apply. Processing solely on our behalf differs from disclosure for a recipient's own purposes; we assess actual arrangements and applicable safeguards. Contact us about arrangements relevant to your request. We do not sell player information.

## Security and retention

We use access controls, session protections, encrypted OAuth tokens and protected audit records. No internet service guarantees absolute security. See the [Cookie Notice](https://docs.devdylnz.com/legal/cookies/) for essential cookies and browser storage.

We retain information for justified competition operation, historical results, disputes, accountability, privacy requests, security, recovery and legal obligations. Protected agreement and notice evidence is not a justification for indefinite retention. A correction may need an explanatory record or controlled remedy instead of silently rewriting historical evidence.

Existing privacy administration supports assessed requests and retention review. Review periods are not promises of automatic deletion. Conditional scoresheet cleanup applies only to eligible files no earlier than 90 days after official completion, subject to holds, configuration and operational checks; it is not a promise that every record, file or backup disappears on day 90. Backups, provider records and independent Discord copies have different handling. This policy introduces no new deletion system.

## Your rights and concerns

Contact privacy@devdylnz.com to request access, correction, or review of removal/retention, or raise a concern. The relevant server, tournament, team or gaming tag can help locate information. Do not send passwords, tokens or identity documents. We use proportionate verification and protect other people's information.

We assess requests under applicable law and explain decisions and lawful limits. Removal is not automatic; justified historical records or legal duties may affect the response. If a correction is not made, you may request that a statement of correction be associated where the law provides. You may also contact the [New Zealand Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/).

## Age and changes

The product minimum is 13+, subject to higher applicable legal or Discord requirements. Discord login does not verify age or capacity. We do not routinely collect birth dates or identity documents or establish parental verification through this feature. Additional requirements can apply internationally; this policy is not a worldwide compliance claim.

Material changes will be dated and communicated as appropriate. Earlier legal and agreement versions remain preserved; new wording does not rewrite old evidence.
