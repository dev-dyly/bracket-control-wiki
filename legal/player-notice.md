# Player Information Notice

**Updated: 22 October 2026** · For teammates whose gaming tags are entered by a captain or organiser.

You can be listed in a Bracket Control tournament **without signing in yourself**. A captain or organiser can enter your gaming tag, team membership and optional Discord ID; configured draft waiting rooms may also record participation through Discord.

**Why we collect this:** To register teams, run drafts, arrange matches, record scores and maintain standings and results.

**What others may see:** Authorised captains and organisers see permitted tournament administration details. Your gaming tag, team name, approved roster, brackets, scores, rankings and results **may be public** on tournament pages or in Discord channels. Screenshots and independent Discord copies may remain.

**Who operates it:** Bracket Control / Dev Dyl NZ, a New Zealand sole trader (NZBN **9429054041851**). The service runs at [app.devdylnz.com](https://app.devdylnz.com/app/). Discord, Cloudflare, Railway and other providers support the service and may handle data overseas.

**Your rights:** To ask for access, correction, review of removal or to raise a concern, email **privacy@devdylnz.com**. Include your server or gaming tag where helpful. Do not send passwords or identity documents. Supplying the details is normally voluntary, but an organiser may not be able to include you without a gaming tag.

**Age:** Participation is intended for people **13+**, or older where law or Discord rules require it. Please report concerns about ineligible participants.

See the full [Privacy Policy]({{ '/legal/privacy/' | relative_url }}), [Terms of Service]({{ '/legal/terms/' | relative_url }}) and [Cookie Notice]({{ '/legal/cookies/' | relative_url }}).

**Captains and organisers:** Share this notice with affected players promptly when registering or importing them. Bracket Control also has its own indirect-collection responsibilities; merely posting a link is not always sufficient to meet them.
