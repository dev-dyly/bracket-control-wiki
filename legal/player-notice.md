## Why you may receive this notice

A captain, tournament organiser or authorised administrator may have supplied your information without you signing in. Information may also come from authorised imports, recurring registrations, Discord account/member checks or account and voice-presence information in a configured draft waiting room. This feature does not record voice conversations.

The notice or person sharing it should identify the hosting server, tournament, team/draft pool and actual source. If that context is missing or wrong, ask the sender or contact privacy@devdylnz.com. Opening this public page does not itself mean your roster information has been collected.

## Information and purposes

Depending on participation, we may handle gaming tags, Discord IDs, usernames/display names or nicknames, available avatars, relevant membership/verification information, team/roster membership, draft presence and picks, attendance, scores, standings, results, disputes and permitted match evidence. We retain relevant captain declarations, rules/legal versions, notification attempts/outcomes and any optional receipt or Discord-contact permission.

We use necessary information for registration and account verification, tournament/draft administration, roster approval, matches and results, privacy information and rights requests, security and accountability. We do not assert a statutory power compelling you to supply tournament information.

## Discord identification and competition rules

For new captain-managed rosters in explicitly activated tournaments, the captain's own account is associated through Discord sign-in. The captain searches the hosting server for your username or nickname and selects your account. The application fills your display label/gaming tag and associates your stable Discord User ID automatically; the gaming tag can be edited independently. You do not ordinarily need to supply a numeric ID manually.

If search cannot find the right account, verified Discord User ID entry is a fallback. The backend checks eligible membership in that server; a plausible number is insufficient. Verification failure prevents that captain-managed submission until resolved appropriately. Historical/inactive and authorised organiser/import/draft workflows may follow other policies and lack IDs. Account verification does not prove legal identity, age, capacity or agreement.

Providing information remains a choice, but missing necessary tags or verified account associations can prevent that registration. These are product requirements, not a claim that a law requires ID submission.

Each tournament has its own published rules, separate from Bracket Control's Terms. The applicable exact version should accompany the competition information. Authoritative stored text and earlier versions are preserved; supplementary external content is not captured or verified. Recurring occurrences have their own snapshots. A captain's single statement records their own acceptance and what they supplied and were told; it does not record separate acceptance by you.

## Access, recipients and public information

Authorised captains and organisers access information needed for their community roles. Dev Dyl NZ and authorised support/privacy personnel use information needed to operate the service and handle requests. Discord, Cloudflare, Railway, GitHub Pages for documentation, email and relevant security/recovery providers support these activities and may process information overseas. Actual provider arrangements and safeguards are assessed; contact us about your circumstances.

Team names, gaming tags, approved rosters, brackets, scores, standings, results and historical placements may be public on authorised tournament pages or selected Discord channels. Spectators outside the hosting server may see them and keep copies. Web and Discord visibility are separate. This feature does not publish Discord IDs, private notices or agreement/delivery histories as public tournament records.

## Privacy notices and choices

Where enabled, your private notice is available through an authenticated Bracket Control account with the matching Discord identity. Availability does not establish receipt. Discord privacy DMs require your own explicit permission and separately enabled, permitted delivery; your captain cannot opt you in. Initial-release Discord privacy DMs are disabled. Permission can be withdrawn in account privacy controls for future attempts; this does not remove earlier messages or resend old notices automatically.

Blocks, opt-outs, unavailable accounts and provider errors may prevent delivery. Captains or organisers may therefore supply this information through an appropriate existing channel and declare that they did so. That declaration does not independently prove delivery. Dev Dyl NZ oversees outstanding notices and retains its own applicable responsibilities.

You may optionally acknowledge receipt using your own authenticated account. This records documents and time, not contractual acceptance, proof of reading every document or DM permission. Individual login, receipt acknowledgement and DM permission are not prerequisites for captain registration.

## Your rights, retention and age

Email privacy@devdylnz.com to request access, correction or review of removal/retention, or raise a concern. The server, tournament, team or gaming tag can help locate information. Do not send passwords, tokens or identity documents. We use proportionate verification, protect others' information and explain decisions and lawful limits. Removal is assessed, not automatic; justified historical evidence may remain. If a correction is not made, you may seek an associated statement of correction where the law provides. You may contact the [New Zealand Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/).

We retain information for justified operation, history, disputes, accountability, security, recovery and legal duties. Immutable evidence is not a justification for indefinite retention. Existing assessed privacy/retention processes apply; this notice introduces no new automatic deletion system. Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://docs.devdylnz.com/legal/privacy/).

The product minimum is 13+, subject to higher applicable legal or Discord requirements. Discord sign-in does not verify age. We do not routinely collect birth dates or identity documents or introduce parental verification through this feature. Other local requirements may apply; no worldwide compliance claim is made.
