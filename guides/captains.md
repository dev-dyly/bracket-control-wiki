# Captain Dashboard

Sign in with the Discord account assigned as your team’s captain, then open [Captain Dashboard](https://app.devdylnz.com/app/my-matches). Your team, tournament and next task appear together.

Use **Your tournament** to switch between your teams and events. The dashboard first opens an event needing attention and keeps your choice while you work. Existing captain links still work.

## Know what needs attention

Check **What’s next** at the top. It tells you when to pick a player, confirm participation, report a result or review an opponent’s report. When you are waiting, it says so.

Your roster and participation status are separate. An approved lineup does not confirm attendance. Each teammate must also complete their own registration and tournament acceptance when required; your captain declaration does not do this for them.

## Confirm participation

1. Choose the tournament you intend to play.
2. Select **Confirm participation**.
3. If the team editor opens, review your lineup and complete any required declarations, then confirm participation there.
4. Check that attendance says **Confirmed**.

An unchanged lineup can retain approval. Changed lineups still need organiser approval. Opening a captain link does not check your team in.

## Draft your team

1. Choose your draft tournament. If you are not listed as captain, ask the organiser to check your assignment.
2. Check whose turn it is. Available players and your selected team appear together.
3. When **Your turn to pick** appears, select a player. Player buttons are disabled while another captain is picking.
4. Follow the round and pick counts. Choose four starters to join you, then up to two substitutes. Use **Pass optional picks** when offered if you do not need more players.

**Standard (1-for-1)** keeps the same captain order each round. **Snake Draft** reverses it each round. Older drafts keep their displayed order. The organiser chooses the style; captains follow the turns shown.

Completed valid draft rosters are approved automatically. See [Draft tournaments]({{ '/guides/drafts/' | relative_url }}) for voice rooms and organiser setup.

## Request a substitute after a draft

1. Open your completed draft tournament in **Captain Dashboard** and select **Request Substitute**.
2. Choose the **Team member to replace**. You cannot replace the designated captain here.
3. Choose your **Preferred reserve player**, then select **Submit Request**.
4. Share the **My Participation** link shown beside the request with the selected player. They must sign in with their own Discord account, confirm the invitation and complete any required Player Registration and tournament rules.
5. Check the request status here. An administrator approves or rejects it. Your current lineup stays in place until the replacement completes.

The chosen player is reserved for your request, so another team cannot request them at the same time. Your team can have one open request. Select **Cancel request** before choosing someone else. Rejection, cancellation or the player's decline makes the reserve available again.

Approval and confirmation complete the replacement when all checks pass. A team cannot change players during an active match, score review or dispute. If the request is still waiting after rules acceptance or a match, ask the organiser to select **Finish replacement**. Completed changes update your team and preserve earlier results.

The Reserve Player Pool contains undrafted players from this tournament. It is created automatically when drafting ends; players are never assigned from it automatically. Only players already linked to a Discord account can be selected. Imported names alone do not verify identity.

## Report or confirm a result

1. Find your opponent under **Active matches**.
2. If your team won the current game, upload one scoresheet and select **Report Win**. Use PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF, up to **10 MB**.
3. If the opponent reported a win, open their scoresheet and select **Confirm Result**, or enter a reason and select **Dispute Result**.
4. Wait for confirmation before reporting another game. A dispute waits for organiser review.

Confirmation does not require another upload. Older matches use **Report result** and require both scoresheets and the series score, as shown on the form. Keep following those requirements.

Completed matches move into **Match History**. Expand **Game history and scoresheets** for earlier games, evidence and corrections. See [Match results]({{ '/guides/match-results/' | relative_url }}) for details.

## Edit team members

1. Under **Your roster**, select **Edit team members** when available.
2. Update the lineup in the editor on the same dashboard. Use the Discord member search when shown.
3. Review the required information and select **Submit roster for approval**.
4. Close the editor when finished. Attendance stays separate from roster changes.

Keep five starters including yourself, with up to two substitutes. If editing is closed, contact your organiser.

## More Options

Expand **More Options** for less frequent tasks:

- **Withdraw from this tournament** skips this event. Read and confirm the withdrawal; an organiser must reinstate it if you change your mind.
- **Recurring participation** controls future automatic entries. Leaving a recurring competition is separate from skipping one tournament.
- **Transfer captaincy or retire your team** opens the existing team management page. A replacement captain must accept and an organiser must approve the handover. Retirement requires typing the team name and preserves team history.
- **View bracket** opens the public tournament view.

To register a new team, follow your organiser’s invitation and the [Team registration guide]({{ '/guides/team-registration/' | relative_url }}). For missing access or blocked actions, see [Troubleshooting]({{ '/troubleshooting/' | relative_url }}).
