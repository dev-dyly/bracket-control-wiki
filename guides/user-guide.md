# Bracket Control user guide

Bracket Control helps communities manage tournaments, teams, drafts and results. Organisers communicate and enforce their own community and tournament rules.

## Browse and review platform documents

Public tournament information can be viewed without Discord sign-in. On your first visit, a compact popup links the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Player Information Notice. Agree to the Terms and separately acknowledge the two notices, then choose **Accept & Continue**. You can choose **Decline / browse** or close it and continue browsing.

Anonymous choices are remembered in that browser. Signed-in choices are recorded against your Discord account; existing acknowledgements for the current document versions are recognised. Updated legal documents require a fresh review. See [Legal acknowledgement]({{ '/guides/legal-acknowledgement/' | relative_url }}).

There is no separate Global Player Registration, registered-player Discord role or registration verification. Individual tournament acceptance is no longer required.

## Administrators

1. Sign in with Discord, connect your server and open Server Setup.
2. Choose Automatic or Manual Setup. Every channel is optional; configure only the features you use.
3. Create a Standard Team or Draft tournament, choose its format, schedule and optional rules, and confirm the configuration.
4. For standard teams, review and approve or reject applications. For drafts, select captains and complete Live Draft.
5. Check attendance, approved lineups, team order and readiness. Generate the bracket when ready.
6. Record or review match results and disputes. Publish to Discord where configured.

See [Server setup]({{ '/guides/server-setup/' | relative_url }}), [Tournament creation]({{ '/guides/tournament-creation/' | relative_url }}), [Administrator guide]({{ '/guides/organisers/' | relative_url }}) and [Draft guide]({{ '/guides/drafts/' | relative_url }}).

## Captains and players

Captains use their organiser's invitation to register teams. Sign in as the team's captain, enter five starters and up to two substitutes, check the roster and submit. New or changed rosters need organiser approval. Returning teams still confirm attendance for each event they intend to play.

Use Captain Dashboard for roster changes, draft picks, reporting wins and confirming or disputing results. Each game needs its own scoresheet or screenshot. See [Team registration]({{ '/guides/team-registration/' | relative_url }}), [Captain guide]({{ '/guides/captains/' | relative_url }}) and [Match results]({{ '/guides/match-results/' | relative_url }}).

A reserve player can respond to a captain-selected substitute invitation under **Profile → Substitute invitations**. Confirming a specific team replacement is separate from legal or tournament-rules acceptance. Organiser approval and existing roster/match checks remain required.

## Support and privacy

Use My Support Tickets to follow your tickets. Support staff access does not grant tournament-administrator access. Notifications remain available from the bell at the top of the page. Privacy Settings retains notices and privacy information; historical records remain available for authorised privacy and audit work.
