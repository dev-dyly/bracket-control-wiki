# Register or update a team

Bracket Control displays a simple [website legal acknowledgement]({{ "/guides/legal-acknowledgement/" | relative_url }}). Global Player Registration and Individual Player Acceptance are no longer required. All Discord setup channels are optional. Tournament organisers communicate and enforce their own rules.

Use this guide for a ready-made team. For captains choosing individual players, use [Draft tournaments]({{ '/guides/drafts/' | relative_url }}).


## 1. Open the correct invitation

1. Open the tournament's registration message or link shared by the organiser.
2. Sign in with the Discord account that will captain the team.
3. Check the server, tournament and registration deadline.
4. Choose **Create a new team** or **Update current team**.

If you cannot find an existing team, check that you used its captain's account and selected the right tournament.

## 2. Enter the lineup

1. Enter the team name for a new team.
2. Check your captain entry.
3. Add four other starters, making five starters including you.
4. Add up to two optional substitutes.
5. Search for each teammate's Discord account in the hosting server and select the correct member.
6. Check each gaming name. If search cannot find the right person, ask the player or server administrator for their Discord user ID and follow the fallback offered on the form.
7. Open **Review proposed roster before submission** and check everyone.

Do not select a similar-looking account just to finish the form.

## 3. Review and submit

1. Review any tournament rules the organiser provides.
2. Share the required platform documents, player notice and rules with your teammates.
3. Check the proposed roster.
4. Select **Register Team**.
5. Check the team's status in **Captain Dashboard**.


## 4. Returning teams and changes

1. Open **Captain Dashboard** and choose the upcoming event.
2. Check its lineup, deadline and attendance.
3. Select **Confirm participation** for the event you intend to play.
4. For a player change, select **Edit team members**, review the lineup and select **Submit roster for approval**.
5. Check approval and attendance separately after any change.

A carried-forward, unchanged lineup can retain approval. It still needs attendance confirmation. **Withdraw from this tournament** skips one event; **LEAVE RECURRING COMPETITION** stops future entries.
