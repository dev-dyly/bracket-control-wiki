# Player participation

You can browse public tournaments without Discord sign-in. Bracket Control shows a compact [website legal acknowledgement]({{ '/guides/legal-acknowledgement/' | relative_url }}) linking its Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Player Information Notice. You can accept or dismiss it. A browser-only choice does not verify your identity.

There is no separate Global Player Registration, Discord registered-player role or Individual Player Acceptance. You do not need to accept each tournament's rules through the website. Organisers communicate and enforce their own community and tournament rules; read the information they share.

For a standard team tournament, your captain submits the roster and the organiser reviews new or changed lineups. For a draft, follow the organiser's instructions for the player pool; captains select players in Live Draft. Discord voice is optional. Weekly teams still confirm their attendance through their captain.

For a captain-selected reserve request, use **Profile → Substitute invitations** and your own Discord account to confirm the specific invitation to join that team. Organiser approval, membership, roster and match-safety checks still apply. This does not involve accepting tournament rules or registering globally.

Captain dashboards, roster management, drafting, substitutes, brackets, match reports, results, Discord publishing and support continue normally. Existing historical legal and participation records are preserved for authorised privacy and audit purposes.

See [Team registration]({{ '/guides/team-registration/' | relative_url }}), [Draft tournaments]({{ '/guides/drafts/' | relative_url }}) and [Captain guide]({{ '/guides/captains/' | relative_url }}).
