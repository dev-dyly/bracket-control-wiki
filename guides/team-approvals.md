# Approve or reject team registrations

**For:** Authorised tournament organisers · **Reviewed:** 29 October 2026.

**This guide is based on the current application's Teams approval UI and approval/readiness documentation.** No dedicated approval screenshots were included in the supplied PDFs.

1. Sign in with an authorised organiser account and choose the correct **Server** and tournament.
2. Open **Dashboard → Manage Tournament → Teams**. The **Overview** may display a **Review registrations** shortcut and a pending count.
3. Find **Approval queue**. Use **Filter applications** to view **pending**, **approved**, **rejected**, **withdrawn** or **all**. Check the counts before deciding.
4. Open each applicant team. Check its name, **captain Discord ID**, roster revision and every starter/substitute. Compare **Previous completed week** with the current roster; new players may be marked **Changed**.
5. Check whether the captain and team identities are appropriate for this server, the division is correct, the starting lineup is complete, and the entry is within the registration and team-cap limits.
6. For a pending, eligible fixed-team application while the tournament is editable, select **Approve**. Or select **Reject**, enter the reason where prompted, and confirm. Avoid approving a mismatched or unverified roster merely to clear the queue.
7. Expand **Approval history** to verify the recorded revision, decision, actor and time.
8. Return to **Overview → readiness**. A saved approval does **not** automatically confirm captain attendance, settle division holds or satisfy personal tournament rules acceptance.

### Approval rules worth knowing

- **Approval is specific to the roster revision.** An edited lineup normally returns to staff review. The previous approved snapshot remains historical evidence and must not be silently replaced in public output.
- **Returning weekly teams:** A safely carried, unchanged approved roster can retain its previous approval. The captain must still **CONFIRM PARTICIPATION** for the new occurrence; a modified roster needs review.
- **Draft tournaments:** Completed and valid web-draft rosters are automatically approved by the dedicated draft workflow. Do not use the ordinary fixed-team approval process in place of completing the draft.
- **Individual player acceptance:** Where enabled, all listed players must personally satisfy the relevant global registration and tournament rules requirements. Organiser approval is a separate condition.
- **Bracket generation:** Requires the complete eligibility/readiness assessment, not simply an Approved badge.

If a registration is not visible, confirm the selected event, application filter, Discord account and registration invitation. Use **Teams → Review & Resolve** for a current recurring/division conflict rather than trying to override it through an unrelated action.
