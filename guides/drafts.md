# Draft tournaments

A draft tournament starts with individual players. Captains choose players to build their teams before the matches begin.

## For players

1. Open Discord and join the **Draft Waiting Room** voice channel for your tournament.
2. Stay in that room until the organiser starts the draft.
3. When the teams are chosen, Bracket Control can move connected players into their team's voice channel.

Your organiser should also tell you how to complete [one-time player registration](https://app.devdylnz.com/app/register) if you have not already done so.

## For organisers

1. Create a **draft tournament**.
2. Make sure the **Draft Waiting Room** is set up in Discord.
3. Open **Manage Tournament → Captain Selection** and select the captains.
4. Bracket Control creates a separate voice room for those captains.
5. Open **Live Draft** and start the player selection when everyone is ready.
6. When the draft finishes, check the teams and create the bracket.

If Bracket Control cannot create a voice room or move a connected player, check that the bot has permission to manage the tournament voice channels.

## For captains

1. Sign in to Bracket Control.
2. Open the draft page your organiser gives you.
3. Wait until you see **YOUR TURN TO PICK**.
4. Click the button next to an available player.
5. Keep choosing players when it is your turn. If you are allowed to skip extra picks, use **PASS REMAINING OPTIONAL PICKS**.

The draft normally gives captains turns in groups of **two picks, then one pick, then two picks, then one pick**, until the required teams are filled.

**You do not need to drag and drop players or make selections by voice.** Captains choose players on the website.

When the tournament ends, Bracket Control removes temporary voice rooms when it can do so safely. If other people are still using a room, it may stay in place until it can be cleaned up.
