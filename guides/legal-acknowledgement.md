# Website legal acknowledgement

Bracket Control displays a compact popup linking the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Player Information Notice.

1. Open the links to review the documents.
2. Agree to the Terms of Service and separately acknowledge the Privacy Policy and Player Information Notice.
3. Select **Accept & Continue**.

You can choose **Decline / browse** or close the popup. Public tournaments remain accessible without Discord authentication. Dismissing the popup does not record agreement.

Without sign-in, acknowledgement is remembered only in that browser. It does not verify who you are. Clearing browser storage or using a different browser can show the popup again. When signed in, acknowledgement is recorded with your existing Discord identity. Previous recorded acknowledgements for the same applicable documents are recognised. If the legal documents change, the popup asks you to review them again.

Agreeing to Terms is separate from acknowledging the two information notices. These acknowledgements are not consent to unrelated data processing. Privacy Settings retains player notices and privacy guidance.

Global Player Registration, Discord registration roles and channels, and Individual Player Acceptance are no longer required. Tournament rules remain optional information. Organisers communicate and enforce their own rules. Team registration, approvals, attendance, drafts, substitutes, brackets, match reporting, results, Discord publishing and support continue normally.

Existing legal, registration and participation records remain preserved for authorised privacy, compliance and audit purposes.
